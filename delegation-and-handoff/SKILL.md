# Delegation and handoff

## Delegation contract
Include only the information needed by the receiving specialist:
- Objective and expected outcome.
- Scope and relevant file/component locations.
- Verified context and findings, with source references.
- Applicable requirements, decisions, and constraints.
- Dependencies, assumptions, and permissions.
- Required deliverable and completion criteria.

## Handoff procedure
1. State what was completed and what remains.
2. Include findings and evidence, not merely a summary of conclusions.
3. Identify the next owner by responsibility.
4. Avoid asking the next agent to repeat completed investigation.
5. Do not pass unsupported assumptions as facts.
6. Keep handoffs concise; link or point to existing artefacts rather than copying them wholesale.
