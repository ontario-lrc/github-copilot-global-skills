# Structured reporting

## Default summary
Use only relevant headings:
- Objective and scope.
- Work performed or decision made.
- Findings or changes.
- Evidence and validation.
- Limitations and unresolved risks.
- Next action or approval required.

## Formal reports
Use a formal template only when the user requests one or when scope, risk, governance, or auditability warrants it. Do not force every small task into a lengthy report. Keep severity, confidence, and status definitions consistent within a report. Never claim tests, reviews, scans, approvals, or compliance checks that did not occur.
