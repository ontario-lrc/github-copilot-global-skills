# Requirements analysis

## Procedure
1. Establish the problem, desired outcome, stakeholders, actors, and scope.
2. Separate functional, non-functional, data, interface, integration, and operational needs.
3. Identify business rules, constraints, dependencies, exclusions, and assumptions.
4. Distinguish mandatory requirements from preferences.
5. Resolve material ambiguity with the user; do not invent stakeholder intent.
6. Write requirements in clear, testable language.
7. Define observable acceptance criteria, including negative, alternate, boundary, and exception cases where relevant.
8. Maintain identifiers and traceability when the project requires them.
9. Record open decisions and confirm readiness for design or implementation.
