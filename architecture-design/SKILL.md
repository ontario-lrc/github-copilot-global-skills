# Architecture design

## Procedure
1. Confirm requirements, quality attributes, constraints, and current-state architecture.
2. Identify design drivers, affected components, trust boundaries, and external contracts.
3. Develop viable options and compare benefits, costs, risks, and operational consequences.
4. Recommend the least complex option that meets requirements.
5. Define responsibilities, interfaces, dependencies, data flows, persistence, and failure handling.
6. Consider security, privacy, reliability, scalability, performance, observability, and maintainability.
7. Identify compatibility, deployment, migration, and rollback implications.
8. Record material decisions as ADRs.
9. Provide implementation and test guidance; list unresolved decisions.
